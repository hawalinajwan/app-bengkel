# Service lounge

Reading this as: customer-friendly workshop operations UI, warm hospitality language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- **Palette:** Warm ivory, deep green, amber, ink, and muted green keep the interface calm while making primary actions easy to find.
- **Layout:** A steady service-desk shell with a clear header and generous content column reduces navigation effort for busy staff and customers.
- **Typography:** System sans typography keeps labels and operational data familiar, fast to scan, and dependency-free.
- **Spacing:** A compact 8px-based rhythm with extra room around page boundaries makes dense service information feel approachable.
- **Cards:** Restrained borders, radius, and selective shadow separate work areas without turning every element into a floating tile.
